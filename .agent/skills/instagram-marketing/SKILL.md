# Instagram Marketing Expert

This skill guides the creation of high-impact Instagram content designed to stop the scroll, build community, and drive conversion.

## 1. Caption Architecture

Instagram captions must be structured for maximum retention. The algorithm tracks "time spent," so lengthy, engaging captions (micro-blogging) often perform well if structured correctly.

### The Hook (Critical)
The first 125 characters are the only thing visible before "more". You MUST catch attention here.
- **Controversial**: "Stop doing X if you want Y."
- **Curiosity**: "I can't believe I didn't learn this sooner..."
- **Direct Benefit**: "How to get [result] without [pain point]."
- **Story**: "It was 3am and I was staring at the ceiling..."
- **Identification**: "Calling all [target audience]..."

### The Value (The "Meat")
Deliver on the hook's promise. Use line breaks to make it readable.
- **Educational**: 3 actionable tips (use emojis as bullets).
- **Emotional**: Vulnerable storytelling or relatable struggle.
- **Entertaining**: Witty observation or humorous take.

### The Call to Action (CTA)
Tell the user exactly what to do.
- **Engagement**: "Save this for later," "Share with a friend who needs this."
- **Conversation**: "Drop a ❤️ if you agree," "What's your take on X?"
- **Conversion**: "Link in bio to read more," "DM me 'READY' to start."

## 2. Copywriting Formulas

Use these frameworks to ensure persuasive writing:

### AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action)
- **Attention**: The Hook.
- **Interest**: Why should they care? (Stats, facts, empathy).
- **Desire**: Paint a picture of the "after" state (Success, relief).
- **Action**: The CTA.

### PAS (Problem - Agitate - Solution)
- **Problem**: Identify a specific pain point.
- **Agitate**: Twist the knife – describe the frustration/cost of staying there.
- **Solution**: Present your content/product as the answer.

### BAB (Before - After - Bridge)
- **Before**: Their current struggle.
- **After**: Their desired paradise.
- **Bridge**: How your content gets them there.

## 3. Hashtag Strategy

Don't just use #love or #instagood. Use a tiered strategy (approx. 15-30 tags).

1.  **Niche Specific (Small)**: 10k-100k posts. High intent. (e.g., #shadowworkjournal, #somatichealing)
2.  **Community (Medium)**: 100k-500k posts. (e.g., #journalingcommunity, #healingjourney)
3.  **Broad (Large)**: 500k+ posts. Reach. (e.g., #mentalhealth, #selfcare)
4.  **Branded**: Your specific tag. (e.g., #TheFeltSenseSeries)

**Placement**: Put hashtags in the caption separated by line breaks (dots) or in the first comment.

## 4. Visual Content Types & Best Practices

- **Carousels (Educational/Story)**:
    - Slide 1: Strong Headline + Visual Hook (1080x1350).
    - Slide 2-8: The Value (Tips, steps, story arc).
    - Slide 9: Summary/Key Takeaway.
    - Slide 10: "Was this helpful? Save/Share" graphic.
- **Reels (Reach)**:
    - 9:16 aspect ratio.
    - Audio: Trending audio (check arrow icon).
    - Text overlay: Hook visible within 4:5 safe zone.
    - Caption: Adds depth to the video.
- **Single Image (Aesthetic)**:
    - High-quality, emotive imagery.
    - 4:5 aspect ratio (1080x1350) takes up more screen real estate.

## 5. Engagement & Growth Tactics

- **The "3-2-1" Strategy**: Before posting, like 3 posts from your niche, comment on 2, share 1.
- **Reply to Comments**: Reply with a question to start a thread (doubles comment count).
- **Stories**: Use Polls, Questions, and Sliders to train the algorithm that people interact with you.

When asked to write a post, output in this format:

**[Format]**: Carousel / Reel / Single Image.

**[Caption]**:
[HOOK]
The 3 lies your anxiety tells you (and how to silence them) 🤫

[BODY]
We often mistake fear for intuition. But somatic psychology tells us...

[CTA]

**[Hashtags]**:
